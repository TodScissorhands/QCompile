How to read this result

These are fixed educational benchmarks, not a general molecular-integral engine. Hamiltonians and physical-sector references are documented and frozen for each model.

The electronic Hamiltonian excludes nuclear repulsion. Total Born–Oppenheimer energy is electronic energy plus the nuclear-repulsion constant, added exactly once. The simulator evaluates exact state-vector expectations; it does not use hardware shots.
